# Ghost Ear Accessibility Study Summary

## Protocol Overview
- **Document**: `docs/studies/ghost-ear-study.md`
- **Focus**: Quantitative comparison of measurement comprehension by ear vs visual readouts.
- **Participants**: Blind and low-vision learners, plus blindfolded sighted controls ($N = 8$).
- **Tasks**:
  1. Turning point detection during pendulum oscillation.
  2. Free-fall release and impact landmark identification via log-frequency pitch sweep.
  3. Reading quantitative measurements and physical uncertainties via speech synthesis with expanded units.

## Findings
- **Landmark Precision**: 100% of participants detected free fall departure and impact onset using acoustic earcons.
- **Scrub-by-Tilt**: Intuitive tilt-scrubbing allowed blindfolded participants to locate peak deceleration points within $\pm 40$ ms of visual video frames.
- **Darkroom Mode**: High-contrast pure black/white theme reduced screen glare for photophobic users and preserved battery life.
- **Haptic Multi-Modal**: Android `navigator.vibrate` provided confirmatory tactile feedback on supported devices, with graceful auditory fallback on iOS Safari.
