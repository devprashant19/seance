# Lesson Plan: Kinematics of Free Fall (40 Minutes)

## Overview & Curriculum Alignment
- **Grade Level**: 9–12 Physics
- **Concept**: Uniform gravitational acceleration, zero-g free fall transition ($h = \frac{1}{2}gt^2$), sensor latency vs acoustic impact detection.
- **Instrument**: Séance Poltergeist Lab (`/lab/free-fall` or `/hear`).

## Student Lab Roles
1. **The Conjuror**: Performs phone drop onto soft foam or cushion from height $h \in [0.5, 1.5]$ m.
2. **The Sensor Warden**: Prepares landing surface and verifies free-fall threshold trigger.
3. **The Scribe**: Records drop height $h \pm \Delta h$, impact time $t_{fall} \pm \sigma$, and submits result to Séance Circle.
4. **The Skeptic**: Inspects accelerometer trace on the Smoked Drum to confirm clean 0-g weightlessness before impact.

## 40-Minute Timeline
- **00:00 – 05:00 (Introduction)**: Weightlessness during free fall. What does the accelerometer measure when dropped?
- **05:00 – 15:00 (Trial Drops onto Cushion)**:
  - Set drop height $h = 1.00 \pm 0.01$ m above foam cushion.
  - Release phone flat without spin.
  - Measure fall duration $t_{fall}$ between free-fall departure ($|a| < 2\text{ m/s}^2$) and impact spike.
- **15:00 – 25:00 (Height Variation)**:
  - Collect drops across 3 heights ($0.6$ m, $1.0$ m, $1.4$ m).
  - Verify $h \propto t^2$.
- **25:00 – 35:00 (Séance Circle Aggregation)**:
  - Review class distribution of measured $g = 2h / t^2$.
  - Evaluate dominant terms in error budget: height uncertainty vs impact timing resolution.
- **35:00 – 40:00 (Debrief)**:
  - Clean up cushion zones; review sensor latency corrections.

## Safety Note
Never drop phones onto hard surfaces. Always use a soft foam pad, bed pillow, or dense cushion.
