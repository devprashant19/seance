# Requirements — Example Tilt Experiment

## Introduction
Measure the phone's tilt angle from its accelerometer's gravity components. A simple first experiment to verify your Seance setup works.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1 — Tilt measurement
1. THE SYSTEM SHALL compute tilt angle theta = arcsin(a_axis / g) from the accelerometer channel.
2. THE SYSTEM SHALL report theta in degrees with standard error of the mean as uncertainty.
